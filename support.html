School Safe QR

Secure student check-in, real-time parent updates, and smarter school attendance tracking.

App Description

About School Safe QR

School Safe QR is a simple and secure student check-in system designed to help schools, parents, guardians, teachers, and school staff stay connected throughout the school day.

With School Safe QR, students can use a secure QR code for school check-in and check-out. Parents and guardians can receive timely updates when their child arrives at school or leaves the campus, giving them better visibility and peace of mind.

The app is designed to support safer and more organized student attendance tracking for schools. It helps reduce manual checking, improves communication with parents, and makes student arrival and departure monitoring easier to manage.

Key Features

Secure Student QR Code

Each student can be assigned a unique QR code for check-in and check-out. This helps schools confirm student movement quickly and accurately.

Parent and Guardian Dashboard

Parents can view linked children, student information, recent activity, attendance status, and school updates in one simple dashboard.

Check-In and Check-Out Updates

Parents can receive updates when their child is checked in or checked out from school.

Student Profile and QR Access

View student details, grade level, section, school information, and assigned QR code in one place.

Teacher and School Staff Tools

Teachers and authorized school staff can view attendance activity, student status, and important school updates.

School Announcements and Events

Schools can share announcements, orientation schedules, family day events, sports day reminders, reading month activities, and other school-related updates.

Attendance History

Parents and school users can review student check-in and check-out history for better tracking and monitoring.

Simple and Friendly Interface

School Safe QR is built with a clean and easy-to-use design for parents, guardians, teachers, and school staff.

Safer School Communication

School Safe QR helps improve communication between schools and families by making student arrival updates faster, clearer, and easier to manage.

Whether you are a parent checking your child’s school status or a school staff member managing student check-ins, School Safe QR provides a convenient way to support safer and smarter school attendance tracking.
