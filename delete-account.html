Account Deletion Request

Delete Your SchoolSafe QR Account

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This page explains how users can request deletion of their SchoolSafe QR account and what happens after an account deletion request is submitted.

Important: Account deletion requests may require identity verification to protect student, parent, guardian, and school records from unauthorized deletion.

1. Who Can Request Account Deletion?

The following users may request account deletion:

  • Parents or guardians with a registered SchoolSafe QR account
  • School staff accounts, subject to school administrator approval
  • Other registered users who no longer wish to use the app

2. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, please send an email to our support team using the email address below:

Email: support@schoolsafeqr.com

Request Account Deletion

In your email, please include the following information:

  1. Your full name
  2. Your registered email address or mobile number
  3. Your school name, if applicable
  4. A short statement saying that you want to delete your SchoolSafe QR account

3. What Happens After You Submit a Request?

After receiving your request, we may contact you to verify your identity. This step helps prevent unauthorized users from deleting accounts that do not belong to them.

Once your request is verified, we will process the deletion of your account within a reasonable period, subject to applicable school policies, system requirements, and legal obligations.

4. What Data May Be Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following personal account information may be removed or anonymized:

  • Your parent or guardian profile information
  • Your login account information
  • Your contact details, such as email address and mobile number
  • Your notification preferences
  • Your active account access to SchoolSafe QR

5. What Data May Be Retained?

Some records may need to be retained for school, security, audit, legal, or operational purposes. These may include:

  • Student attendance scan logs
  • Check-in and check-out history required by the school
  • Audit logs related to account activity and system security
  • Records required to comply with applicable laws or school policies
  • Data needed to investigate misuse, fraud, or security incidents
Please note: Deleting your account may remove your ability to view linked children, receive student check-in/check-out notifications, access scan history, and use other SchoolSafe QR features.

6. Linked Student Records

Deleting a parent or guardian account does not automatically delete official student records maintained by the school. Student records may remain under the control of the school or institution responsible for those records.

7. Cancelling a Deletion Request

If you submitted an account deletion request by mistake, please contact us immediately. We may be able to cancel the request if the deletion has not yet been completed.

8. Contact Us

For account deletion requests or related questions, please contact:

Email: support@schoolsafeqr.com
