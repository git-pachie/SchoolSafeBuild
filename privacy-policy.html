Account Deletion Support

Eligible Parent/Guardian users can permanently delete their SchoolSafe QR account directly within the application.

To access the account deletion feature:

  1. Sign in to SchoolSafe QR.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion information.
  6. Select Delete My Account.
  7. Confirm permanent account deletion.

After successful deletion, applicable account-specific personal information is deleted or anonymized, authentication credentials and active sessions are revoked, notification/device tokens are removed, and the user is signed out.

Certain school records, such as student attendance, check-in/check-out history, QR scan activity, safety records, and applicable audit records, may be retained where necessary for legitimate school operations, safeguarding, security, legal requirements, or records-retention obligations.

If you are unable to access the in-app deletion feature or need assistance with your deletion request, contact:

support@schoolsafeqr.com

Before Contacting Support

To help us understand and resolve your issue, please include the following information when applicable:

  • Your name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your school name
  • Your account role: Parent/Guardian, Teacher, or School User
  • A clear description of the issue
  • The steps you performed before the problem occurred
  • A screenshot of the issue, if appropriate
  • Your device model and operating system version, if relevant

Please do not send your password, authentication code, or other security credentials by email.

About SchoolSafe QR

SchoolSafe QR is designed for participating schools and their authorized parents, guardians, teachers, and school personnel.

The application provides features including student QR-based check-in and check-out, attendance information, scan history, school alerts, acknowledgements, school news and events, student information, and account management.

Available functionality may vary depending on the user's role, participating school, school configuration, and enabled services.
